Outgrowing Myself: The Journey of Becoming a Better Person

Personal growth requires the courage to leave behind old habits, challenge familiar beliefs and embrace the person you have the potential to become.

By: Clementine

Listen to the podcast. 


 
            I want to challenge my own thinking,  
            step outside familiar spaces  
            and give myself permission to try things  
            I once thought were beyond my reach.

            I no longer want to be limited by the version of myself  
            who was afraid to begin.  
            I want to build, create, discover and experience.  
            I want to make mistakes without losing confidence  
            and celebrate progress without waiting for perfection.

            There is still so much I want to learn.  
            So many places I want to explore.  
            So many skills I want to develop  
            and so many versions of myself I have yet to discover.

            I am not in a race to become someone overnight.  
            I am simply committed to becoming better  
            than I was yesterday.

            And perhaps that is what growing up really means:  
            understanding that you are allowed to change,  
            that you are allowed to outgrow your old ways  
            and that becoming yourself is a lifelong journey.

            I honour the person I was.  
            I embrace the person I am.  
            And I look forward to meeting the person I am becoming.
